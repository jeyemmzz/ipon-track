========================================================================

                  IPONTRACK - USER INSTRUCTIONS & GUIDE

========================================================================



Welcome to IponTrack! This lightweight daily budget & savings tracking app 

is designed to help you manage your daily "baon", track expenses, and view 

your weekly savings history across all your devices (PC, tablet, mobile).



------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. HOW TO LOG DAILY BUDGET & EXPENSES

------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Select the target day of the week (Monday - Sunday) using the dropdown.

2. Enter your total allowance/budget for that day in the 

   "Day's Budget / Baon (₱)" field (e.g., 500).

3. Enter what you spent on in the "Item Description" field 

   (e.g., Jeepney fare, Lunch, Coffee).

4. Enter the amount spent in the "Spent Amount (₱)" field (e.g., 150).

5. Click "+ Save Entry".



Note: 

- You can add multiple expenses to the same day.

- If you only want to set a starting budget for the day without an expense 

  yet, leave the description and spent amount blank.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. VIEWING & NAVIGATING HISTORY

------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Use the top toolbar dropdowns to select the Month, Week Number (Week 1–5), 

  and Year you want to view or edit.

- The dashboard automatically displays:

    * Total Weekly Budget: Combined baon/budget logged for that week.

    * Total Spent: Total sum of expenses logged for that week.

    * Total Money On Hand: Your remaining balance (Budget minus Spent).

- Days will only show up in the history list once you log a budget or 

  an expense for them to keep your dashboard clean.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. DELETING AN EXPENSE

------------------------------------------------------------------------

- To remove an item from your list, click the red trash icon next to the 

  logged item amount in the breakdown section.

- The dashboard totals will automatically recalculate.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. DATA SAVING & PRIVACY

------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Your data is stored locally on your device's web browser using LocalStorage.

- No external server or login is required.

- Data stays saved on your browser even if you refresh or close the tab.

- To keep your data intact, avoid clearing your browser's site data/cookies 

  for this site.
